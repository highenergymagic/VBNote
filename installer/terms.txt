VBNote

VBNote is an emulator of the HumanWare VoiceNote QT and BrailleNote mPower.
It exists because these machines are no longer made, cannot be repaired
indefinitely, and the people who rely on them are running out of spares.

Before installing, two things need saying. Both are conditions of using it.

1. THIS IS NOT A HUMANWARE PRODUCT.

   It is an independent project. It is not affiliated with HumanWare, not
   endorsed by them, and not supported by them. Nothing about it should be
   presented as theirs.

2. DO NOT CONTACT HUMANWARE ABOUT IT.

   They did not make it and cannot support it. Questions about VBNote belong
   with the VBNote project, at
   https://github.com/highenergymagic/VBNote

VBNote does not include the machine's firmware and cannot obtain it for you.
Setting up a machine needs the bootloader and operating system from a machine
you own. That firmware remains its owner's property; this software neither
copies nor distributes it.
